【难句解析】

1. Climate change 101 (Title)

【原文】Climate change 101

【翻译】气候变化入门

【解析】在美国大学课程编号系统中，101通常用于标记某一领域或主题入门级的基础课程。本文作者运用101代表文章中所包含的信息是理解气候变化这一主题所需的最基本信息。


2. Since global temperature records began in the late 19th century, the planet's average surface temperature has risen by approximately 2.0°F (1.1°C), with 16 of the 17 hottest years on record occurring over the last 35 years. (Para. 2)

【原文】Since global temperature records began in the late 19th century, the planet's average surface temperature has risen by approximately 2.0°F (1.1°C), with 16 of the 17 hottest years on record occurring over the last 35 years.

【翻译】自19世纪末开始有全球温度记录以来，地球表面平均温度已上升约2.0华氏度（1.1摄氏度），在有记录的17个最热年份中，有16个出现在过去的35年里。

【解析】此句结构比较复杂，since 引导时间状语从句，表示“自……以来”；主句使用现在完成时 has risen，与 since 引导的表示一段时间的状语连用；主句之后是 with 引导的伴随状语，现在分词 occurring 强调 occur 这一动作或状态与主句状态同时进行。


3. Eighty percent of this additional heat is absorbed by the world's oceans, the surface of which has increased in temperature by 0.13°F per decade since 1901 (NASA, n.d.). (Para. 2)

【原文】Eighty percent of this additional heat is absorbed by the world's oceans, the surface of which has increased in temperature by 0.13°F per decade since 1901 (NASA, n.d.).

【翻译】这些增加的热量中有80%被地球上的海洋吸收，自1901年以来，海洋表面温度每十年升高0.13华氏度（美国航空航天局，未注明日期）。

【解析】n.d. 为 no date 的缩写。在学术写作中若年代不明，常用 n.d. 标注。


4. Ice ages—driven by slow variations in the Earth's orbit that alter the distribution of the Sun's energy—have occurred, on average, every 100,000 years. (Para. 3)

【原文】Ice ages—driven by slow variations in the Earth's orbit that alter the distribution of the Sun's energy—have occurred, on average, every 100,000 years.

【翻译】冰河期——由地球轨道的缓慢变化所驱动，这种变化改变了太阳能量的分布——平均每十万年发生一次。

【解析】此句主句为 Ice ages have occurred every 100,000 years. driven 引导的过去分词短语作后置定语，相当于定语从句 which were driven ... 修饰 Ice ages 解释说明其形成的原因。句中破折号主要表示停顿，目的是把 driven 引导的插入成分隔开。


5. A blanket of greenhouse gases—carbon dioxide, water vapor, nitrous oxide, and methane—in the Earth's atmosphere absorb heat and warm the planet's surface, a natural phenomenon known as the greenhouse effect. (Para. 4)

【原文】A blanket of greenhouse gases—carbon dioxide, water vapor, nitrous oxide, and methane—in the Earth's atmosphere absorb heat and warm the planet's surface, a natural phenomenon known as the greenhouse effect.

【翻译】地球大气中的大量温室气体——二氧化碳、水蒸气、氧化亚氮和甲烷——吸收热量并使地球表面变暖，这种自然现象被称为温室效应。

【解析】此句中破折号的作用是用来解释名词短语 A blanket of greenhouse gases 的。本句后半部分为 known 引导的过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰名词短语 a natural phenomenon。


6. Since the early 1800s, ocean acidity has increased by around 25%, depressing the metabolic rates of some species, lowering the immune responses of others, destabilizing marine ecosystems, and causing the bleaching, and possibly the eventual death, of the world's coral reefs. (Para. 5)

【原文】Since the early 1800s, ocean acidity has increased by around 25%, depressing the metabolic rates of some species, lowering the immune responses of others, destabilizing marine ecosystems, and causing the bleaching, and possibly the eventual death, of the world's coral reefs.

【翻译】自19世纪初以来，海洋酸度增加了约25%，抑制了某些物种的代谢率，降低了其他物种的免疫反应，破坏了海洋生态系统，也造成全球珊瑚礁白化，甚至有可能最终死去。

【解析】此句中，主句使用现在完成时表示动作或状态从过去某时开始，持续到现在，还可能持续下去；主句后四个现在分词短语并列，在句中作结果状语，说明海水酸度上升对海洋物种的影响，此处并列短语的使用有加强语气的作用。


7. Although the recent resolution reached in Paris is hugely significant, many claim it is too little, too late. (Para. 8)

【原文】Although the recent resolution reached in Paris is hugely significant, many claim it is too little, too late.

【翻译】尽管最近在巴黎达成的决议意义重大，但许多人认为这太微不足道、太迟了。

【解析】此句中“在巴黎达成的决议”是指《巴黎协定》(Paris Agreement)，旨在大幅减少全球温室气体排放，将全球气温升幅限制在比工业化前水平高2°C以内，并寻求将气温升幅进一步限制在1.5°C以内的措施。

【难句解析】

1. This increase arises through economies of scale, as the growth in labor in the city allows firms to produce more output while their fixed costs remain largely the same. (Para. 2)

【原文】This increase arises through economies of scale, as the growth in labor in the city allows firms to produce more output while their fixed costs remain largely the same.

【翻译】这种增长通过规模经济实现，因为在固定成本基本不变的情况下，城市劳动力的增加能使企业提高产出。

【解析】economies of scale: 规模经济（因生产规模扩大而成本降低）。


2. Buildings require less electrical heating than those in more remote places due to typically higher temperatures in urban areas and buildings which lock those temperatures in. (Para. 3)

【原文】Buildings require less electrical heating than those in more remote places due to typically higher temperatures in urban areas and buildings which lock those temperatures in.

【翻译】由于城市地区温度通常较高，建筑物蓄热锁温，因此建筑物的电力供热需求小于偏远地区。

【解析】此句中 due to 结构将句子分成前后两部分。前半句中 more remote places 等同于 rural areas。后半句中说明了为什么城市建筑比农村地区的建筑需要的电力供热更少，即城市温度通常更高、城市的建筑锁温蓄热。


3. Public transportation has been proven to use less fuel for every passenger kilometer compared to private transportation (Chester et al., 2010). (Para. 3)

【原文】Public transportation has been proven to use less fuel for every passenger kilometer compared to private transportation (Chester et al., 2010).

【翻译】事实证明，与私人交通相比，公共交通每乘客行驶一公里所消耗的燃料更少（切斯特等，2010）。

【解析】passenger kilometer: 旅客公里，乘客公里（指运输公司用来计算利润水平等的基本单位，其计算方法为：在给定的时间内，以公里为单位，用总旅程距离除以乘客人数）。


4. The sharing economy is one example of innovation that can be profitable and beneficial to people's lives and the environment. (Para. 4)

【原文】The sharing economy is one example of innovation that can be profitable and beneficial to people's lives and the environment.

【翻译】共享经济就是一个例子，这一创新不仅有利可图，也有益于人们的生活和环境。

【解析】sharing economy: 共享经济（人们通常可以通过互联网共享财产、服务等的经济体系）。


5. Some local governments have schemes that allow private individuals or companies to use their facilities when empty (e.g. a school gym), to reduce spare capacity as much as possible. (Para. 4)

【原文】Some local governments have schemes that allow private individuals or companies to use their facilities when empty (e.g. a school gym), to reduce spare capacity as much as possible.

【翻译】一些地方政府制定了计划，允许私人或公司使用闲置中的设施（如学校体育馆），以尽可能减少闲置容量。


6. As no country has ever reached middle or high income status without urbanization, it is evident that urbanization must be encouraged to allow developing countries the opportunity to derive the same benefits as developed countries, i.e. an improved standard of living. (Para. 5)

【原文】As no country has ever reached middle or high income status without urbanization, it is evident that urbanization must be encouraged to allow developing countries the opportunity to derive the same benefits as developed countries, i.e. an improved standard of living.

【翻译】没有任何国家未经城市化就达到了中等或高收入水平，所以很显然，必须鼓励城市化，使发展中国家能够有机会获得与发达国家同样的好处，即提高生活水平。

【解析】此句为主从复合句。As 引导原因状语从句，其中用了双重否定（no... without...）表肯定，相当于 for every country that has reached middle or high income status, they have made it with urbanization。在主句中，it 为形式主语，真正主语为 that 引导的从句；allow 后面接双宾语，即 developing countries 和 the opportunity；动词不定式 to derive the same benefits as developed countries 作后置定语，补充说明 the opportunity；an improved standard of living 呼应 benefits。


7. Social mobility will increase for all; something that no one can deny is a desirable goal. (Para. 5)

【原文】Social mobility will increase for all; something that no one can deny is a desirable goal.

【翻译】所有人的社会流动性都将增加；没有人能否认这是一个理想的愿景。

【解析】此句句式较为特殊。分号连接两个逻辑关系紧密的分句，something 指代分号前的分句。分号后的分句中，that no one can deny 是同位语从句，修饰 something。整句话可理解为：Social mobility will increase for all. This is a desirable goal. No one can deny it.

【难句解析】

1. Instead, he developed a theory of anthropological criminology stating not only that criminality was inherited, but that criminals could be identified by a series of prominent physical defects which confirmed their atavistic and savage nature. (Para. 2)

【原文】Instead, he developed a theory of anthropological criminology stating not only that criminality was inherited, but that criminals could be identified by a series of prominent physical defects which confirmed their atavistic and savage nature.

【翻译】相反，他提出了一种犯罪人类学理论，指出犯罪行为不仅具有遗传性，而且人们可以通过一系列显著的身体缺陷来识别罪犯，这些缺陷证实了罪犯天生的返祖性和残暴性。

【解析】此句为主谓宾结构，主干部分为he developed a theory of anthropological criminology，现在分词短语 stating not only that...but that...作定语修饰a theory of anthropological criminology，作用相当于一个定语从句；该分词短语中not only...but (also)...结构连接两个并列的宾语从句，that criminality was inherited and that criminals could be...，第二个宾语从句中又包括一个由which引导的定语从句，修饰physical defects。


2. The notion that physical appearance was innately bound to a propensity toward criminality was furthered by William Sheldon in Atlas of Men (1954), in which he proposed a taxonomy for categorizing the human physique. (Para. 2)

【原文】The notion that physical appearance was innately bound to a propensity toward criminality was furthered by William Sheldon in Atlas of Men (1954), in which he proposed a taxonomy for categorizing the human physique.

【翻译】威廉·谢尔登在《男性图鉴》（1954）中进一步提出了身体外型生来就与犯罪倾向相关的观点，并在书中提出了一种人类体型的分类法。

【解析】此句为主谓宾结构，主干部分为the notion was furthered by William Sheldon，that引导的同位语从句对主语the notion进行解释说明，后半句中which引导的定语从句修饰Atlas of Men。


3. Other early theorists laid the foundations for the most prominent school of thought in the 50s and 60s—"labeling theory," which hypothesizes that negative labels given to individuals by society actually promote deviant behavior. (Para. 3)

【原文】Other early theorists laid the foundations for the most prominent school of thought in the 50s and 60s—"labeling theory," which hypothesizes that negative labels given to individuals by society actually promote deviant behavior.

【翻译】其他早期的理论家为“标签理论”奠定了基础，这是上世纪五六十年代最著名的思想流派，该理论假设社会给予个人的负面标签实际上会促成越轨行为。

【解析】此句破折号后的部分为同位语，对the most prominent school of thought进行解释说明。which引导定语从句修饰labeling theory，其中hypothesizes后面是宾语从句that negative labels...promote deviant behavior，过去分词短语given to individuals by society作定语修饰宾语从句的主语negative labels。

4. While this data, and other studies like it, strongly imply that criminal parents are likely to produce criminal offspring, it remains unclear whether this intergenerational deviance is genetically determined or largely due to the environment in which we are raised. (Para. 4)

【原文】While this data, and other studies like it, strongly imply that criminal parents are likely to produce criminal offspring, it remains unclear whether this intergenerational deviance is genetically determined or largely due to the environment in which we are raised.

【翻译】虽然这些数据以及其他类似的研究有力地表明，犯罪的父母可能会生养出犯罪的子女，但目前尚不清楚这种代际越轨是由基因决定的，还是主要由我们的成长环境决定的。

【解析】此句为主从复合句，while引导让步状语从句，表示“尽管……”；主句中的it为形式主语，真正的主语为whether引导的名词性从句whether this intergenerational deviance is...raised。在该从句中又包括一个由which引导的定语从句，修饰the environment。


5. One area of research that tests this hypothesis compares the behavior of identical (monozygotic) twins—those sharing an identical genetic makeup—to that of fraternal (dizygotic) twins, who share, on average, 50% of the same genes. (Para. 6)

【原文】One area of research that tests this hypothesis compares the behavior of identical (monozygotic) twins—those sharing an identical genetic makeup—to that of fraternal (dizygotic) twins, who share, on average, 50% of the same genes.

【翻译】有研究领域通过比较单卵双生子和异卵双生子的行为来验证这一假说，前者具有相同的基因构造，后者平均拥有50%的相同基因。

【解析】此句可以简化为one area of research compares A to B，A表示the behavior of identical (monozygotic) twins，B表示the behavior of fraternal (dizygotic) twins。具体来看，定语从句that tests this hypothesis修饰本句主语one area of research；在compare A to B结构中，两个破折号之间的内容those sharing an identical genetic makeup是对identical (monozygotic) twins的解释说明；后半句中，第二个破折号后的that指代前半句中的the behavior；who引导的定语从句修饰fraternal (dizygotic) twins，也是对fraternal (dizygotic) twins的解释说明。


6. To blame our genes for criminal behavior willfully ignores a broader societal responsibility to ensure that the environment in which we're raised doesn't promote criminal behavior. (Para. 7)

【原文】To blame our genes for criminal behavior willfully ignores a broader societal responsibility to ensure that the environment in which we're raised doesn't promote criminal behavior.

【翻译】故意将犯罪行为归咎于基因会忽视一份更大的社会责任，那就是确保我们成长的环境不会助长犯罪行为。

【解析】此句主语为动词不定式短语 to blame our genes for criminal behavior，第二个动词不定式短语 to ensure that ... criminal behavior 作定语修饰 societal responsibility，对其进行解释说明；在 that 引导的宾语从句中，还包括一个定语从句 in which we're raised，修饰宾语从句的主语 the environment。

1. Automation forced thousands out of work and free trade agreements curtailed demand as the subsequent globalization and foreign monetary policies meant that coal and steel were cheaper to import than to produce domestically. (Para. 2)

【解析】此句是并列句，由并列连词and连接两个分句。第二个分句while tax reductions ... 中包含一个由while引导的让步状语从句，该分句的主语是they，指代前面的tax reductions，incentive payments和no-interest loans。


4. Towns and cities in the Rust Belt may well benefit from a two-pronged approach, using tourism and commerce to improve the local economy in order to increase the standard of living for the existing population without trying to return to population sizes of the past. (Para. 9)

1. 【原文】Broadly speaking, biomass can be divided into four distinct categories—wood and agricultural products such as logs, sawdust, and agricultural waste; solid waste (i.e. garbage); landfill gas (i.e. methane); and liquid fuels such as biodiesel and ethanol, which are produced from crops such as wheat, corn, sugar beet, and sugar cane.

【翻译】一般来说，生物质可分为四大类——木材和农产品，如原木、锯末和农业废弃物；固体废物（即垃圾）；填埋气（即甲烷）；以及液体燃料，如生物柴油和乙醇，这些燃料由小麦、玉米、甜菜和甘蔗等作物生产。

【解析】此句主干部分为 biomass can be divided into four distinct categories，破折号之后的成分为同位语，用于解释说明 four distinct categories；关系代词 which 引导的定语从句修饰 liquid fuels。需要注意的是，biomass 是本文的核心概念，用 biomass 生产出的能量可被称为 bioenergy。


2. While the effects of burning biomass can be mitigated to a certain extent by choosing to burn biomass that is less polluting, it is highly doubtful that it can ever be classed as truly carbon-neutral. (Para. 4)

【原文】While the effects of burning biomass can be mitigated to a certain extent by choosing to burn biomass that is less polluting, it is highly doubtful that it can ever be classed as truly carbon-neutral.

【翻译】虽然通过选择燃烧污染较小的生物质，可以在一定程度上减轻燃烧生物质的影响，但生物质能否被视为真正的碳中和，仍然非常令人怀疑。

【解析】此句为主从复合句。前半句为 while 引导的让步状语从句，后半句 it is highly doubtful that ... 为主句，其中 it 为形式主语，that 引导的主语从句为真正的主语，that 引导的主语从句中 it 指代 biomass。需要注意的是，carbon-neutral（碳中和的；碳平衡的；碳中性的）指的是组织或机构以植树等方法抵消自身排放的二氧化碳，达到相对的“零排放”。


3. 【原文】These "energy crops" are predominantly inedible and, as they are cultivated on an industrial scale, are associated with the same environmental concerns as any other mass agricultural practice—namely, the pollution of water and soil from pest control, the excessive use of water, and the erosion of soil.

【翻译】这些“能源作物”大多不可食用，而且由于它们以工业规模种植，因此与其他大规模农业实践一样，会带来相同的环境问题——即病虫害防治造成的水和土壤污染、过度用水以及土壤侵蚀。

【解析】此句主干为 These "energy crops" are predominantly inedible and ... are associated with the same environmental concerns。句子中共有两个 as。第一个 as 作从属连词，引导原因状语从句；第二个 as 与 the same 搭配，构成 the same ... as ... 结构。破折号后的内容为 environmental concerns 的同位语，对其作进一步的解释说明。namely 后的三个名词短语是并列结构，最后一个短语前用连词 and。
